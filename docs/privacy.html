Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Offsight Operations Bot ("Bot") processes information when providing Discord access management, verification, and operational functionality.

1. Information We Process

Discord Information

The Bot may process:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username
  • Discord display name
  • Discord server membership
  • Discord roles
  • Discord permissions
  • Information necessary to process interactions with the Bot

Work Information

The Bot may process information contained in approved organizational access records, including:

  • Work email address
  • Name
  • Active or inactive status
  • Team assignment
  • Subteam assignment
  • Workflow access
  • Leadership access
  • Other access-control information configured by administrators

Verification Information

When email verification is used, the Bot may process:

  • Work email address
  • Hashed verification code
  • Verification expiration time
  • Verification attempts
  • Verification status
  • Verification timestamps

Verification codes are temporary and are not intended to be stored in plaintext.

Operational and Security Logs

The Bot may maintain logs containing:

  • Verification events
  • Successful and unsuccessful verification attempts
  • Roles added or removed
  • Access synchronization events
  • Administrative actions
  • Errors
  • Timestamps
  • Discord user IDs
  • Relevant work email addresses

These logs are used for administration, troubleshooting, security, and auditing.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

  • Verify that Discord users are authorized
  • Associate Discord accounts with approved work identities
  • Determine appropriate Discord roles and permissions
  • Provide access to authorized Discord channels
  • Remove access that is no longer authorized
  • Synchronize Discord access with organizational records
  • Prevent unauthorized access
  • Investigate errors or security incidents
  • Maintain administrative audit records
  • Operate Bot workflows and commands
  • Maintain and improve reliability and security

Information processed by the Bot is not sold or used for third-party advertising.

3. Authorization Source

The Bot may use an organization-controlled Google Sheet or another approved organizational system as an authorization source.

The authorization source may contain:

  • Work email address
  • Name
  • Active status
  • Team membership
  • Subteam membership
  • Authorized access levels

Changes to the authorization source may automatically result in Discord roles being added or removed.

4. Email Verification

When verification is requested, the Bot may send a temporary verification code to the submitted work email address.

The Bot checks whether the address is authorized before granting restricted access.

Verification codes expire after a limited period. Verification attempts may be limited or temporarily blocked to protect against abuse.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be processed through service providers necessary for operation of the Bot, including:

  • Discord
  • Google services
  • The configured email provider
  • Infrastructure used to host or operate the Bot

Information may also be accessible to authorized organizational administrators when necessary for access management, security, troubleshooting, auditing, or operation of the Discord server.

Personal information processed by the Bot is not sold.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for:

  • Providing Bot functionality
  • Managing access
  • Maintaining security
  • Investigating incidents
  • Organizational recordkeeping requirements
  • Applicable legal obligations

Temporary verification information is intended to expire after the configured verification period.

Account associations and audit records may be retained for longer periods where reasonably necessary for access administration, security, or auditing.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the Bot.

These measures may include:

  • Restricted administrative permissions
  • Role-based Discord access
  • Temporary verification codes
  • Hashing verification codes rather than storing them in plaintext
  • Protected application credentials
  • Limited access to authorization records
  • Access synchronization
  • Audit logging

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Access, Correction, and Deletion

If information associated with your Discord access is incorrect, contact an administrator.

Where applicable, you may request:

  • Information about personal data maintained about you
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of information that is no longer required

Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate security, organizational, contractual, or legal purposes.

9. Automated Access Management

The Bot may automatically assign or remove Discord roles based on information contained in the organization's authorization source.

Changes to active status, team assignment, subteam assignment, workflow authorization, or leadership authorization may automatically change which Discord channels a user can access.

If you believe an automated access change is incorrect, contact an administrator for review.

10. Discord

The Bot operates using Discord's platform.

Discord separately processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy describes processing performed in connection with Offsight Operations Bot and does not replace Discord's policies.

11. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for authorized organizational users and is not designed or intended for use by children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Bot functionality, data practices, service providers, security practices, or applicable requirements change.

The "Last Updated" date identifies the current version.
