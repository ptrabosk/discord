# Workflows

Start a workflow by typing /workflow in the chat box in any channel. Only you see the form you open, and each step is private to you.

- **Timesheet Adjustment:** fix a missed or incorrect clock-in or clock-out. Choose the adjustment type, the shift date and start time, the end time, and explain what needs correcting. Operations reviews it. You get a DM when it is approved. If it is rejected, the DM tells you the reason and asks you to submit a new Timesheet Adjustment.
- **Emergency Shift Release:** ask to give up a shift you can't work. Choose the date and time and explain why. The request is automatically acknowledged without a confirmation message.
- **Qs & Feedback:** ask a question or send feedback. Choose a category and write your message. Operations can reply to you by DM when needed.
- **Dispute:** dispute a message. Enter the Message ID and explain why you are disputing it. QA team will review as usual and results will be in the QA sheet.
- **Working Location:** tell us where you will be working from if your location changes. Enter the start date, end date, and new location. Type dates as MM/DD/YYYY.

You must be verified to submit. If /workflow says you need to verify, go to the **initial-setup** channel first.
